# Intent router and answer policy

Entry prompt: **“What would you like to explore?”** A user can choose an intent directly; an AI classifier may suggest a mode later, but must show the choice and allow override. The router is not a fortune engine. It selects a method, a time horizon, a question template and safety constraints.

| Intent | Primary method | Output boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Today / this week / month / year | Western | Themes and possibilities, with source/rule and horizon label; no exact event promise. |
| Love / career / study / money in a period | Western horizon + optional Tarot | Topic-specific themes and low-risk preparation. Money remains educational, not investment instruction. |
| “Will I get this job?” | Tarot situation spread | Explore opportunity/obstacle/controllable action; no yes/no certainty or invented recruiter knowledge. |
| “Will my ex return?” | Tarot relationship spread | Reflect on present dynamics and user's choices; no claim to know another person's feelings or intent. |
| “A or B?” | I Ching decision mode | Current state/change/considerations; preserve user agency and real-world criteria. |
| “What is changing over years?” | Western long horizon; optional advanced Vedic | Traditional timing themes, not dated guaranteed events. |
| Lucky color / avoiding conflict / remedy | Western or Tarot according to context | Optional ritual/color prompt or communication action; no promise that it changes luck. |
| “Which deity should I worship?” | Belief exploration, not divination assertion | Ask for tradition/consent; describe options respectfully, avoid declaring a deity objectively assigned or promising worship will improve outcomes. |

## Response contract

Every answer states: **method and horizon; what the method surfaced; a calibrated possible scenario; what is unknown; one or two controllable actions; optional ritual/reflection; source/rule provenance.** The scenario should feel concrete enough to answer the question (e.g. “you may hear back or need to follow up”) without saying an outcome is certain or attaching fake probabilities.

For high-stakes health, legal or financial decisions, keep the fortune layer reflective and direct the user to qualified advice or factual decision criteria. A user asking about employment can still receive practical follow-up and interview preparation because those are low-risk actions. No fear-based upsell or irreversible “remedy.”

## Hybrid routing

The user can combine methods, but each retains its source label and role. Example: a job question may show a Tarot spread, then optional Western daily context. The system must not merge them into a single unsupported certainty score.
