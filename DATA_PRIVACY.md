# Data and privacy — 4.4

Registered users may store only an account ID/provider reference, timezone/language, optional derived Sun sign, reading date/content and source/rule/model versions, and optional feedback. Notification consent/time is production-only. The birth date is used to derive an approximate sign; prefer retaining only the derived sign when exact date is unnecessary. Exact time/place are not collected in the POC.

The expanded full-chart mode may request exact birth date, time and place with a clear purpose. A question mode may store only intent/domain and method result; raw question text is ephemeral by default, saved only by user choice. Tarot draw IDs and I Ching cast states are needed to replay an opted-in reading. Do not infer or retain a user's religious belief from deity questions. A choice to save a tradition preference would require a separate explicit feature and privacy review.

Guest readings use a temporary session and general context with no persistent account/history. Reflection notes are optional and potentially sensitive, private by default and never sent to the narrative model. Avoid contacts, live GPS, messages, health and financial information.

Production needs controlled access, encryption, retention periods, deletion of readings/notes/account and documented consent changes. Retention durations and legal compliance require product/privacy review; they are not asserted here.
