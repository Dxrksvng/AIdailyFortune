# Facts, design choices and open questions

**Official facts:** the system is AI daily fortune-telling; platform/method are unrestricted; answer 4.1–4.5, including blueprint, storage rationale/ERD and KPIs. Source: [official PDF](../assignment-brief/Forward%20Deployed%20AI-Eng%20Assignment%202026.pdf), page 11.

**Our choices:** Daily Compass, responsive web, adult reflection audience, astrology-inspired method, guest general mode, optional date-only Sun-sign mode, versioned rule engine, LLM wording and evening check-in. These are proposals, not Sunday requirements or existing infrastructure.

**Expanded choices after user steering:** day/week/month/year and life-domain coverage; Western/Tarot POC, I Ching MVP and Vedic advanced; intent router; concrete but calibrated scenarios; optional ritual and respectful deity-question handling. The POC only implements a daily-lite lunar rule and 22-card Major Arcana Tarot, not full natal/horizon/I Ching/Vedic engines.

**Open for pilot:** Thai versus multilingual launch, guest retention policy, account demand, notification demand, exact rule set and precision, whether users value the explanation/action/reflection sequence, retention periods and monetization. Answer these with user research rather than invented facts.
