# Safety

Frame the product as entertainment and reflection. Use “may” and “consider”, never guaranteed predictions. Prohibit medical, legal and financial directives, self-harm or dangerous instructions, fatalistic relationship advice, death/disaster certainty and fear-based pressure.

Validate each candidate narrative before display. On failure, use an approved template and log a reason code without private text. Allow content reports, notification opt-out and deletion. High-stakes user requests receive a neutral response encouraging appropriate real-world support.

For employment, relationships, money and study, offer preparation without invented certainty or probabilities. Rituals and lucky colors are optional cultural prompts with no promise of causal benefit. Never urge a user to spend money, forgo treatment, stop applying for work, or change religious practice to avert a predicted misfortune. Deity questions should respect the user's tradition and autonomy; do not assign a faith or promise worship will improve outcomes.
