# Market research — checked 1 October 2026

First-party published pages were inspected. “Observed” describes what the company says is available; “interpretation” is our positioning hypothesis. This is not a hands-on feature audit.

| Product | Observed capabilities | Interpretation / limit | Official source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Co–Star | Birth time/date/location, personal chart, friends/compatibility, daily updates; describes NASA/JPL data, human astrology and natural-language AI. | Already personal and social. No claim that it lacks reflection. | [FAQ](https://www.costarastrology.com/faq/), [About](https://www.costarastrology.com/about) |
| CHANI | Daily horoscopes, personalized transits, meditation, live journal and personalized reflection prompts; says its content is written by astrologers. | Already supports reflection and action-like content. Daily Compass proposes a single tightly linked daily loop. | [App page](https://www.chani.com/app) |
| Faladdin | Describes customized coffee-cup readings from submitted photos and user information; terms list coffee, tarot, astrology and other methods. | Breadth of methods is part of its published offer; no unsupported quality comparison. | [Support](https://www.faladdin.com/support/en), [Terms](https://www.faladdin.com/eula) |

**Proposed position:** “Explainable, actionable, reflective daily guidance.” The claim is about Daily Compass's intended sequence, Signal → Why → Action → Reflection, not exclusivity of individual features or predictive superiority. Validate preference for this sequence through user interviews and a pilot.
